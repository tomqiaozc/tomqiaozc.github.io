01 · Project & system context

Office Plus is not a payment endpoint; it is a set of cooperating commercial domains.

A completed payment is only a transaction fact. The platform must still evaluate campaign eligibility, grant membership or usage entitlements, call third-party services, notify the user, and remain correct as account, contract, or external-system state changes.

Transactions

Orders record the money facts

SKUs, pre-orders, orders, payments, refunds, and invoices describe what happened in a purchase.

Campaigns

Campaigns orchestrate growth rules

Purchase bonuses, eligibility, prize packages, and redemption are separated from order conditions so growth logic does not spread through transaction code.

Entitlements

User services manage assets

Membership duration, Paper Check word quotas, and redemption entitlements have distinct issuance, consumption, and rollback rules.

Messaging

Async work advances long-running flows

Background jobs and Service Bus handle payment callbacks, checks, renewals, notifications, and failure compensation.

Identity

Accounts and devices control access

AuthenticationCenter manages login, Tokens, device sessions, revocation, and expiration cleanup.

Team foundation

My initiatives reuse platform capabilities

Payment channels, entitlements, campaigns, messaging, Blob, and configuration platforms were built by the team and underpin the five initiatives below.

02 · Core system constraint

A successful order does not mean the contract, entitlement, or third-party result also succeeded.

Each domain must persist its own state and converge through idempotency, retries, and compensation. Compressing every step into one synchronous request turns any external timeout into an unknowable partial success.

Correctness

Locks reduce contention; state and uniqueness decide

Callbacks, messages, and scheduled jobs may all run more than once. Business state machines, unique keys, and conditional database updates jointly prevent duplicate deductions or entitlement grants.

Recoverability

Every failure has an explicit next step

Network failures, third-party rejection, inconsistent results, and interrupted jobs enter retry, query, rollback, refund, or compensation paths instead of being swallowed as one generic exception.

Personal contribution 01 · Paper Check commercialization

The hard part was not calling the Wanfang API, but turning the capability into a complete product.

I led the overall design, core development, cross-service and cross-team coordination, and launch. Payment channels, messaging, and Blob reused team infrastructure; my work connected files, orders, usage entitlements, third-party checking, and failure refunds into an operable loop.

From file upload to result delivery

One user request is split into five traceable, recoverable stages.

End-to-end lead
File boundary

The Add-in uploads directly to Blob with a short-lived SAS. The business service stores only the file path, metadata, and business state, keeping large files out of the application layer.

Personal contribution 02 · WeChat recurring subscription

Recurring subscription is not a monthly cron job; it is long-term consistency across four kinds of state.

I led the design and implementation. A contract only proves that the user authorized recurring deductions. The first month and every renewal period still need an independent order, payment callback handling, and membership entitlement issuance so each amount, payment result, and entitlement source remains auditable.

Model first-month signing separately from later renewal

Contract, monthly order, payment, and membership entitlement each persist their own state.

End-to-end lead
First month

A short-lived PreContract stores signing context. After verifying the WeChat signing callback, the system persists the contract asynchronously, creates the first-month order, and requests a deduction. Only a successful deduction writes Payment, advances the order, and calls AddVip.

Renewal

A reminder job sends an in-product message in advance. The renewal job pages through Signed contracts nearing expiry, creates the current month’s AUTO_RENEWAL order, and requests the deduction.

Duplicate handling

A unique contract number, callback signature verification, RedLock, the order state machine, a recent-renewal-order check, and delayed cancellation jointly handle duplicate callbacks and job reruns.

Failure handling

A failed first deduction releases the coupon and terminates the empty contract. User cancellation and WeChat deletion callbacks advance independent contract states instead of using order state as a proxy for the contract fact.

Personal contribution 03 · Account soft deletion

Replace immediate physical deletion with a reversible, auditable account lifecycle.

I led the overall design, core development, cross-service coordination, and launch. The goal was to raise the cost of repeatedly deleting or merging accounts to obtain discounted entitlements while preserving a cooling-off period and recovery path for legitimate users.

Persist deletion intent, then let a periodic job finish cleanup

Deletion is not one delayed message; it is a business event that can be scanned again.

End-to-end lead
Precondition

Deletion is rejected while a WeChat recurring contract exists, requiring cancellation first so WeChat cannot continue deductions after the account disappears.

Soft delete

The account enters DELETING, creates a unique deletion event, and revokes old Refresh Tokens. State and uniqueness constraints block duplicate requests.

Recovery

Logging in during the cooling-off period restores ACTIVE, removes the deletion event, and clears caches. Account state is checked server-side before a new Token is issued.

Final cleanup

A Worker periodically pages through expired events and checks state again before physical deletion. One item’s failure does not block the batch; the event remains for the next scan.

Honest boundary: The state machine, unique event, pre-delete recheck, periodic rescan, and cross-service deletion replay are shipped. Strict restore/delete mutual exclusion, backend revalidation of M365 conditions, and durable compensation for failed Token revocation can still be strengthened.

Personal contribution 04 · Token & device sessions

Use JWT to reduce per-Token state, then retain revocation through device records.

I led the Refresh Token redesign, device-session model, device-count limit, cleanup Worker, load-test-based selection, and versioned migration. The core question was not simply “switch to JWT,” but what could be stateless and what the server still had to persist.

Personal contribution 05 · Partner top-up

Separate “request accepted,” “VIP granted,” and “notification delivered” into three outcomes.

I led the Partner model, external API, security boundary, asynchronous fulfillment, callback compensation, queries, and refund loop. When a partner tops up VIP by phone number, the external request is untrusted and the notification network is unreliable, but one order must never grant the entitlement twice.

Acceptance

A synchronous response only means the request entered the system

After validating application identity, permission, business signature, SKU, callback URL, and duplicate order, the service creates an INIT record and publishes a message. It cannot yet claim that VIP has arrived.

Fulfillment

TopupStatus records the business result

The Worker creates an order and calls the entitlement service; only completion of the local flow moves the record to SUCCESS. A unique partner-order constraint plus state checks prevents duplicate top-up.

Notification

NotifyStatus is persisted independently

A notification failure does not roll back an already granted VIP and must not rerun top-up. The partner can query actual state, and the platform can resend notification separately.

Security

Layer identity, permission, and parameter integrity

Entra application identity and App Role control who may enter the API, the Partner Secret signature protects this order’s parameters, and HTTPS provides transport confidentiality.

Personal contribution · Membership orders & standard payments

Understanding a complex legacy system does not imply ownership of its original design.

I did not lead the team’s existing standard order-and-payment architecture. I contributed to parts of it later and mapped the Growth service, SKU display, pre-order/order, WeChat and Alipay payment callbacks, refunds, and exception compensation. WeChat recurring subscription was a separate initiative that I led.

Transaction model

SKUs and orders are not static page data

Displayed offers combine on-sale state, user type, order history, current entitlement or contract state, and campaign gifts. Temporary PreOrder and persistent Order serve different lifecycles.

Payment correctness

Callback state and channel facts decide together

Callbacks enter background jobs that use an order lock, state machine, and uniqueness constraints to prevent duplication. Before timing out an order, the system queries the payment channel’s actual state so a delayed callback does not cause a false closure.

Refund recovery

Money and entitlements settle separately, then converge

A refund request, asynchronous channel result, order/payment update, and VIP revocation are separate steps that require queries, delayed jobs, and compensation.

My boundary

Contribute implementation and build a system mental model

This case shows that I can locate state, idempotency, and compensation points in a complex legacy system. I do not package the team’s original architecture, payment channels, or Campaign platform as my own design.

09 · Engineering trade-offs & boundaries

These initiatives produced four practical engineering conclusions.

Domain state

Do not represent an entire flow with one table

Contracts, orders, payments, entitlements, third-party tasks, and notifications each persist their own facts so failures can be located precisely.

Idempotency

Locks, state machines, and uniqueness have different jobs

Locks reduce contention, state machines constrain legal transitions, and durable uniqueness provides the final decision. One distributed lock is not the whole correctness model.

Recovery

Failure is a business branch, not the end of a log

Retry, query, rollback, refund, rescan, and manual handling must be designed explicitly and leave durable state that can be evaluated again.

Fact boundary

Separate shipped work, contribution, and improvements

A cross-instance billing-period unique key and strict soft-delete mutual exclusion are improvement directions. Standard payment was a later contribution and must not be mixed with the five led initiatives.
