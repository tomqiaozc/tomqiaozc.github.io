01 · Project & system context

Office Plus is not a payment API; it is a set of cooperating commercial domains.

A user completing payment is only a transaction fact. The platform must still evaluate campaign eligibility, grant membership or usage entitlements, call third-party services, notify users, and stay correct when account, contract, or external-system state changes.

Transaction

Orders record the money fact

SKUs, pre-orders, orders, payments, refunds, and invoices describe what happened in a purchase.

Campaign

Campaigns orchestrate growth rules

Buy-gift, eligibility, prize packs, and redemption are separated from order conditions so growth logic does not scatter across transaction code.

Entitlement

User services manage assets

Membership duration, Paper Check word counts, and redemption entitlements have different grant, consumption, and rollback rules.

Messaging

Async tasks advance long chains

Background tasks and Service Bus carry payment callbacks, detection, renewal, notifications, and failure compensation.

Identity

Accounts and devices control access

AuthenticationCenter manages login, tokens, device sessions, revocation, and expiry cleanup.

Team foundation

Personal work reuses platform capabilities

Payment channels, entitlements, campaigns, messaging, Blob, and configuration platforms are built by the team and form the basis for the four owned workstreams below.

02 · Core system constraint

Order success does not mean the contract, entitlement, or third-party result all succeeded.

Each domain must keep its own state and converge through idempotency, retries, and compensation. Cramming every step into one synchronous request turns any external timeout into an undecidable “half success.”

Correctness

Locks smooth peaks; state and unique constraints arbitrate

Callbacks, messages, and scheduled tasks may all run repeatedly; business state machines, unique keys, and conditional database updates jointly prevent duplicate charges or grants.

Recoverability

Every failure has an explicit next step

Network failure, third-party rejection, result inconsistency, and task interruption each go to retry, query, rollback, refund, or compensation rather than being swallowed uniformly.

Owned workstream 02 · WeChat recurring subscription

A recurring subscription is not a monthly scheduled task; it is long-term consistency across four kinds of state.

I owned the design and rollout. A contract only represents the user's authorization to auto-debit; the first month and each renewal cycle still create an independent order, handle the payment callback, and grant membership, so every amount, payment result, and entitlement source is auditable.

Model first-month sign-up and subsequent renewals separately

The contract, monthly order, payment, and membership entitlement each keep their own state.

End-to-end led
First month

A short-lived PreContract holds sign-up context; after verifying the WeChat sign-up callback, the contract is persisted asynchronously, then the first-month order is created and charged. Only after a successful charge are the Payment written, the order advanced, and membership granted.

Renewal

The reminder task sends in-app notifications ahead of time; the renewal task pages through soon-to-expire Signed contracts, creates this month's AUTO_RENEWAL order, then requests a charge.

Duplicate handling

Contract-number uniqueness, callback signature verification, RedLock, the order state machine, a latest-renewal-order check, and delayed cancellation jointly handle duplicate callbacks and task reruns.

Failure handling

A failed first charge releases the coupon and terminates the empty contract; voluntary cancellation and WeChat deletion callbacks advance through a separate contract state, never using the order state to replace the contract fact.

Owned workstream 03 · Account soft deletion

Turn “immediate physical deletion” into a reversible, auditable account lifecycle.

I owned the overall design, core development, cross-service delivery, and launch. The goal is both to raise the cost of repeatedly deleting / merging accounts to obtain discounted entitlements, and to give real users a cooling-off period and a restore path.

Persist the deletion intent, then let a periodic task complete the cleanup

Deletion is not a delayed message; it is a business event that can be re-scanned.

End-to-end led
Up-front block

Deletion is rejected while a WeChat recurring-subscription contract exists; the user must cancel first, so WeChat does not keep charging after the account disappears.

Soft deletion

The account enters DELETING, a unique deletion event is created, and old Refresh Tokens are revoked; duplicate requests are blocked by state and unique constraints.

Restore

Re-login during the cooling-off period restores ACTIVE, removes the deletion event, and clears caches; account state is the server-side decision before issuing a new token.

Final cleanup

A Worker periodically pages through expired events and re-checks state before physical deletion; a single failure does not block the batch, and the event stays for the next round.

Owned workstream 05 · Partner top-up

Split “request accepted, VIP delivered, notification sent” into three results.

I owned the Partner system, external API, security boundaries, async fulfillment, callback compensation, query, and refund loop. When a partner tops up VIP by phone number, the external request is untrusted and the notification network is unreliable, but the same order must never grant entitlements twice.

Accept

A synchronous response only means entry into the system

After validating app identity, permissions, business signature, SKU, callback URL, and duplicate orders, create an INIT record and enqueue a message; VIP must not be claimed as delivered yet.

Fulfill

TopupStatus records the business result

A Worker creates the order and calls the entitlement service; only after the local flow completes does it enter SUCCESS. The partner order unique constraint and state check prevent duplicate top-ups.

Notify

NotifyStatus is stored separately

Notification failure neither rolls back an already-granted VIP nor re-runs the top-up; the partner can query the real state, and the platform can resend notifications alone.

Security

Identity, permissions, and argument integrity are layered

Entra app identity and App Roles control who can call the API; the Partner Secret signature protects this order's arguments; HTTPS protects transport confidentiality.

Transaction modeling

SKUs and orders are not static page data

Displayed results stack on top of on-sale state, user type, order history, current entitlement / contract, and campaign gifts; the temporary PreOrder and the persisted Order carry different lifecycles.

Payment correctness

Callback state and channel facts jointly adjudicate

Callbacks enter background tasks and use order locks, state machines, and unique constraints to prevent duplicates; before timeout close, the real channel state is queried to avoid mis-closing due to callback delay.

09 · Engineering trade-offs & boundaries

These workstreams formed four engineering principles.

Domain state

Do not use one table for the whole chain

Contracts, orders, payments, entitlements, third-party tasks, and notifications each keep their own facts so you can tell where a failure happened.

Idempotency

Locks, state machines, and unique constraints have distinct roles

Locks reduce contention, state machines restrict legal transitions, and persistent unique constraints provide the final arbitration; a distributed lock is not the whole correctness story.

Recovery

Failure is a business branch, not the end of a log line

Retry, query, rollback, refund, backfill, and manual handling must be defined at design time and leave persisted state that can be re-decided.

Fact boundary

Distinguish shipped, contributed, and improvable

Cross-instance unique keys for recurring subscriptions and strict soft-delete mutual exclusion are improvements; standard payments are a later contribution and must not be conflated with the four owned workstreams.
